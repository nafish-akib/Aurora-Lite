Privacy Policy — Aurora Browser

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Aurora Browser ("the app") is a web browser designed for Android TV. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

We do not create user accounts, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

  • Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, and saved passwords are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us.
  • Weather feature: to show local weather on the home screen, the app determines your approximate location. This uses either your device's last known location (with your permission) or IP-based geolocation. Your location (or IP-derived location) is sent to third-party services only to fetch weather data: Open-Meteo (weather data), ipapi.co / ipinfo.io / ifconfig.co (IP-based location lookup). These services receive a standard web request containing your IP address, as any website visit would.
  • Websites you visit may set cookies and other site data in the browser, exactly as in any web browser. You can clear this at any time in Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet: required to browse the web.
  • Location: used only for the optional weather card. You can decline it.
  • Storage: used to save downloads and exported files on your device or USB drives.
  • Notifications: used for download progress/completion alerts.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone. Third-party weather services receive only the minimum request needed to return weather for your location. If you do not use the weather card, no location data is ever requested.

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also clear browsing data at any time via Settings → Clear Browsing History.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
